A1 French – Beginner

From zero to your first real conversations. Target: NCLC 1–3.

  • Duration: 8–12 weeks
  • Online · group or private
  • NCLC 1–3
  1. A1NCLC 1–3
  2. A2NCLC 3–4
  3. B1NCLC 5–6
  4. B2NCLC 7+

Is A1 for you?

  • You have never studied French, or only know a few words.
  • You studied French in school years ago and forgot most of it.
  • You want a solid base before aiming for NCLC 7.

What you'll learn

  • The French sound system and pronunciation that Canadians understand
  • Present tense of the most useful verbs (être, avoir, aller, faire…)
  • Introducing yourself, your job, your family and your city
  • Numbers, dates, times, prices and directions
  • Short everyday dialogues: shopping, appointments, transport
  • Reading short messages, signs and simple forms

At the end of A1, you can…

Introduce yourself and talk about your daily routine

Understand slow, clear speech on familiar topics

Fill in a simple form and write a short message

Handle basic interactions at a store, clinic or office

Format & duration

Duration: 8–12 weeks per level, depending on your rhythm.

Formats: small group (evenings / weekends) or private classes, 100% online.

Price: see pricing.
