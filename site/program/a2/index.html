A2 French – Elementary

Talk about your past, your plans and your life in Canada. Target: NCLC 3–4.

  • Duration: 8–12 weeks
  • Online · group or private
  • NCLC 3–4
  1. A1NCLC 1–3
  2. A2NCLC 3–4
  3. B1NCLC 5–6
  4. B2NCLC 7+

Is A2 for you?

  • You finished A1 or can already handle very basic French.
  • You understand slow French but freeze when you need to answer.
  • You want to move from words to full sentences.

What you'll learn

  • Past tenses: passé composé and imparfait
  • Near future and talking about plans
  • Describing people, places, your work and your experience
  • Making requests, invitations and simple complaints
  • Understanding short announcements and voicemails
  • Writing short emails and personal messages

At the end of A2, you can…

Tell what you did last weekend or in your last job

Understand the main point of short, clear audio

Write a short, structured email

Take part in simple conversations without preparing

Format & duration

Duration: 8–12 weeks per level, depending on your rhythm.

Formats: small group (evenings / weekends) or private classes, 100% online.

Price: see pricing.
