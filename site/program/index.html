The French4PR program: zero to B2 in 4 steps

One path, four levels, one goal: NCLC 7 in all four skills on TEF Canada or TCF Canada. You start where you are today, and you always know the next step.

  1. A1NCLC 1–3
    BeginnerIntroduce yourself, ask simple questions, understand everyday basics.
    8–12 weeks
  2. A2NCLC 3–4
    ElementaryDescribe your experience, handle daily tasks, tell simple stories.
    8–12 weeks
  3. B1NCLC 5–6
    IntermediateGive opinions, explain problems, understand longer audio and texts.
    8–12 weeks
  4. B2NCLC 7+
    Upper-IntermediateAim for NCLC 7 in all 4 skills on TEF Canada or TCF Canada.
    8–12 weeks

Two formats, same path

Small group classes

Learn in a group of 5 to 8 future permanent residents, all on the same goal. Evenings and weekends. The most affordable way to progress, from $239/month.

Private classes

One-on-one, at your pace, on your schedule. Ideal if your permit expires soon or your test date is already booked.

What a typical class looks like

Warm-up

Short speaking round on a real-life topic. Everyone talks.

New skill

One clear grammar or vocabulary point, explained in English when needed.

Practice

Listening, reading and writing tasks modelled on the test.

Feedback

Personal corrections and homework to keep progressing between classes.
