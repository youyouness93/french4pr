B1 French – Intermediate

Express your opinion and start working on test-style tasks. Target: NCLC 5–6.

  • Duration: 8–12 weeks
  • Online · group or private
  • NCLC 5–6
  1. A1NCLC 1–3
  2. A2NCLC 3–4
  3. B1NCLC 5–6
  4. B2NCLC 7+

Is B1 for you?

  • You finished A2 or you can hold a simple conversation.
  • You want to start practising TEF/TCF-style tasks.
  • You need more fluency and confidence when you speak.

What you'll learn

  • Giving and defending an opinion
  • Conditional, subjunctive basics and complex sentences
  • Connectors to structure a speech or a text
  • Understanding radio, interviews and longer texts
  • Writing articles, letters and short argued texts
  • First timed practice on the 4 test skills

At the end of B1, you can…

Explain a problem and suggest a solution

Follow the main ideas of a radio segment or article

Write a clear, structured text of 150–200 words

Speak for a few minutes on a familiar topic

Format & duration

Duration: 8–12 weeks per level, depending on your rhythm.

Formats: small group (evenings / weekends) or private classes, 100% online.

Price: see pricing.
