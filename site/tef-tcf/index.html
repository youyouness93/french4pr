TEF Canada & TCF Canada: the French tests for Express Entry

Both tests are accepted by IRCC for permanent residence. To qualify for most French-language Express Entry draws, you need NCLC 7 or higher in all four skills. Here's what that means.

  • TEF Canada
  • TCF Canada
  • Objective: NCLC 7

The 4 skills you are tested on

Each skill gets its own NCLC level. Your weakest skill decides whether you reach NCLC 7 "in all four", so we train all of them.

Listening

Compréhension orale: understand conversations, announcements, interviews.

Reading

Compréhension écrite: understand messages, articles and documents.

Writing

Expression écrite: write messages, stories and argued texts.

Speaking

Expression orale: get information, convince and debate with an examiner.

Both tests lead to the same NCLC levels, and IRCC treats them the same. The real differences are the format of each task, the test dates available near you, and which format suits your strengths. We help you choose during your assessment and train you on the format you pick.

How to register for the test

  • Find a TEF Canada centre on the Le français des affaires website.
  • Find a TCF Canada centre on the France Éducation international website.
  • Read the official IRCC language test requirements.
  • Book early: popular centres in the Greater Toronto Area fill up weeks ahead.
